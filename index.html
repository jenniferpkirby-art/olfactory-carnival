01 · THE COLLECTION RULE

One sheet, one collection

This build uses your six-column Google Sheet only: House, Fragrance, Carnival Line, Key Notes / Source Description, Inferred Accord Families (for pairing), and Personal Impressions (Condensed).

What gets used in the app

  • Every fragrance row in the connected sheet is eligible for Spin the Wheel, Choose Your Poison, and Wild Card.
  • No size, travel, sample, or attar distinctions are required.
  • No ratings, wear history, or writeback are used.

If the live Google Sheet is unavailable, the included backup collection remains available automatically.

How to get the correct link

  4. Publish, copy the URL, and paste it above.
